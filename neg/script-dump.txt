== HOW THE GAME WORKS (context for reviewers)
Starting meters: Customer trust 60, Delivery 70, Team trust 55, CEO time 80 (0-100).
UVA on: every negative meter change is multiplied by 0.6. UVA off: by 1.3. Positive changes are the same.
Manu runs always start with committed=1 (Bruna said yes before telling him).
Scene order: call, channel, story, heat (only if someone is still angry), joao (only if someone escalated; with UVA on, UVA runs separate sessions instead and João's office is skipped), ask 1, ask 2, build the deal, customer, ending. Choosing Power or Rights in João's office jumps straight to the ending.
Chapter 3: the option picked in ask 1 is hidden in ask 2.
Build the deal (no dialogue): the player sets tonnes next week and the week after (steps of 5) and can tick: ask for a six-month forecast, offer priority planning, weekend overtime (+15 t safe capacity, quality risk), partner grower (+20 t, lower margin). Safe capacity is 40 t. UVA only shows "Does every side clear its line?" there and never proposes numbers. The other director then reacts in one line (the first line of Chapter 4).
Taking back a confirmed hundred (committed=1, less than 100 t next week) costs Customer trust 6 on top of the plan's own effect.
otherFactKnown means the player has learned the other side's key fact (Bruna: Manu's 40 t limit and forecast need; Manu: the long-term account). channel=text means the player chose the text message in Chapter 2.
